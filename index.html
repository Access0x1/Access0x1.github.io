What it is

One shared router serves every merchant. A payment is priced USD-to-token inside the settlement transaction via a Chainlink feed, an exact fee is split atomically (net + fee == gross, proven under fuzz), and net value lands at the merchant in the same tx. The contract never holds merchant funds.

  • Payments — non-custodial, USD-priced, exact fee split, all in one tx
  • Commerce — subscriptions, bookings, invoices, gift cards, zero-custody NFT sales
  • Identity — ENS names as payment surfaces, a ○ → ✓ → ✓✓ verification ladder, verified-human checkout
  • Agents — x402 nanopayments under hard budgets; the rail mints the agent's wallet and its name
